# NutriClinic 1.11.0 - released 20 November 2026

Published at [sahnoroloji.org/nutriclinic](https://sahnoroloji.org/nutriclinic/) on 20 November 2026. This revision addresses the clinician's reported immediate target-rate and automatic protein-powder suggestions, adds exact-market formula comparison, and implements the additional calculation and safety corrections approved on 20 November. The [README](../README.md#choosing-formulas) explains the formula-selection workflow.

- A new continuous enteral feed has a separate progressive-start proposal with 12-hour review intervals and first-day totals. These are local protocol examples within the existing phase limits; 48-72 hours is not a universal deadline. Ongoing tolerated feeding, refeeding risk, severe electrolyte deficits and shock retain distinct pathways.
- Phase target rate, actual planned first-day amount and daily product amounts are distinct. Each stage of a two-product proposal gives the amount of each separately administered product. Physical mixing is not assumed.
- Single products and two-product recipes are ranked by remaining energy/protein gaps within current targets and supplied volume limits. The default is shown without requiring a manual choice. Alternatives and manually entered amounts remain visible, with excesses flagged rather than silently applied.
- The reference catalog contains 22 adult products from five manufacturers, with country/package identities and current manufacturer source access dates. It is not an exhaustive global-market inventory. Unverified Turkish Resource Energy composition is explicitly pending; foreign labels are not silently substituted. Unknown free water remains unknown.
- No automatic protein-module prescription is added. Approximate nitrogen balance is descriptive and no longer raises the protein target or labels a definitive anabolic/catabolic state.
- PN amino-acid energy is reconciled with total calories. Refeeding risk is separated from documented severe malnutrition for the early-PN exception.
- FOIS/IDDSI cannot establish oral-feeding safety. Prokinetic alternatives include renal, neurologic, QT and interaction criteria instead of an unconditional repeated drug dose.
- Additional water uses known formula water and explicitly assessed other fluids, restrictions and renal context. Numeric total-fluid references remain visible if these are incomplete. Entered fluid/volume budgets constrain formula suggestions.
- Impossible energy/protein labels are rejected; pump and intermittent volumes preserve phase ceilings. Actual feeding duration is separate from ICU stay.
- Missing critical information retains a numeric conditional proposal, with prominent assumptions and reasons, according to the user's chosen policy. Known contraindications remain distinct.
- Numeric catalog selections survive local/file/server case round trips. Arbitrary text is excluded from anonymous site copies. No new runtime package, installer, dependency download or external calculation service is introduced.
- Print styling prevents a transient screen animation from producing a blank report. Source exports retain the matching license and citation files.

Verification passed 147 Node tests and 12 Python tests locally, followed by 12 server-side synthetic tests in an isolated network-disabled container. Live checks covered a fresh generated owner's formula-catalog persistence and cleanup, protected-route status, EN/TR, phone layout and report output. Existing patient records were not inspected or overwritten; a consistent server-side recovery snapshot was verified before the software update. These are technical checks, not a claim of clinical validation or proof of superiority of any product.

Main primary references: [ESPEN ICU 2023](https://www.espen.org/files/ESPEN-Guidelines/ESPEN_practical_and_partially_revised_guideline_Clinical_nutrition_in_the_intensive_care_unit.pdf), [NICE CG32](https://www.nice.org.uk/guidance/cg32/chapter/recommendations), [ASPEN refeeding consensus](https://doi.org/10.1002/ncp.10474), [FDA metoclopramide label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/017862s64lbl.pdf). Manufacturer references and dates are stored with each catalog entry. The local review retains source-access details and test evidence.
